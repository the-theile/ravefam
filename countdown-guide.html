Pin the hype 📲

Keep your crew's next rave on your home screen, with the day count always in view.

  1. Open the RaveFAM app once while signed in. That loads your crews into the widget.
  2. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, then tap Edit → Add Widget (or +).
  3. Search RaveFAM and pick a size: small, medium, or large for every crew. Tap Add Widget.
  4. Want a specific crew? Long-press the widget → Edit Widget → Crew. Add a few widgets to track a few crews.
  5. Lock screen: long-press the lock screen → Customize → Lock Screen → tap the widget row → RaveFAM.

Needs iOS 17 or later and the latest RaveFAM app from the App Store. The count ticks over at midnight on its own.

No RaveFAM in the widget list?

Until your app updates, the free Scriptable app can show the same countdown as a widget.

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store.
  2. Copy the script below, open Scriptable, tap +, paste it and tap Done.
  3. Add a Scriptable widget to your home screen, long-press it → Edit Widget → Script → pick the one you pasted.
