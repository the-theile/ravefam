01 · Getting in

No passwords.
Just you.

Sign up in under a minute, grab your handle, and claim the spot your crew already saved for you.

📡ScreenshotClaim your spot
✉️Passwordless login

Nothing to forget. Your email or phone number is your key.

How it works: enter your email or number and we send a code. By text it's a code to type in; by email you also get a magic link to tap.

🔑A second way in

Lose access to one? You've still got the other.

How it works: link both your email and your phone number in Settings, and sign in with either.

🏷️Your @handle

Claim the name your crew knows you by.

How it works: pick it during signup. Onboarding then walks you through your sound, your vibe and your first crew.

📷Claim your spot

Your crew lead may have saved you a spot before you ever signed up.

How it works: scan their QR code, open their invite link, or enter their 6-digit invite code. You'll see your details first, then claim the spot as yours.

💚Free for the FAMFREE

Every feature on this page. No card, no catch. On iPhone or right in your browser.

02 · Crews

Your fam's
home base.

Lead your own or join the ones you're invited to, as many as you like. Every crew starts in Secret, so you can build and maintain it quietly.

Secret

The default. Only you can see it while you add your people.

Recruiting

Crew gets notified, QR codes and invite links go live.

Locked In

Circle's complete. Lock it down.

👻Add them before they join

Track your whole fam before anyone's signed up.

How it works: tap Add Member to save someone as an unclaimed profile. They claim it later with your invite.

🔗Invite link + QR

One link, one scan or one code, and they're in.

How it works: flip the crew to Recruiting, then share its QR code, invite link or 6-digit invite code from the crew page.

🗿Totem, name & colour

Make the crew unmistakably yours.

How it works: set a totem photo, then rename and recolour from crew settings. The totem shows on your invites and cards.

✨"Here now" glow

See when the crew's online and planning.

How it works: a crew glows in your list while someone's in its Huddle or Rave Plan. Turn yours off anytime in Privacy.

🎟️Crew Card

Your crew as a shareable rave laminate.

How it works: share the full roster, or just who's going to the next rave, straight to your story.

📍FAM Pins & History

Achievements you earn together, plus a timeline of everything the crew's done.

How it works: pins unlock from real milestones, like your first fest together. Track progress on the crew page.

03 · Huddle

The group chat,
finally sorted.

Real-time chat for the whole crew, plus a dedicated room for every rave you're going to together.

💬Crew + rave rooms

One chat for the crew, one per rave, so plans don't get buried.

How it works: crew rooms are always there. A rave's room opens inside its Rave Plan.

🚨Beacons

For the moments that actually matter. Lost at the fest? Meetup moved?

How it works: tap the beacon button in Huddle to send an urgent ping that cuts through the chat.

↩️Replies, @mentions & GIFs

Everything you'd expect from a group chat, with the crew vibe built in.

🔍Search & jump

"Wait, what time did we say?" Find it in seconds.

How it works: tap 🔍 to search every room, filter by room or person, and jump straight to the message.

📤Share to RaveFAMiOS

Found the perfect reel? Send it to the crew without leaving the app you're in.

How it works: in Instagram, TikTok or Safari, tap Share → RaveFAM and pick the Huddle it goes to.

04 · Rave Plan

Logistics in one place,
not 400 texts.

As soon as you and a crewmate are both going to the same rave, it gets its own Rave Plan: chat, tasks, roles, rides, stays, outfits and the lineup.

✅Tasks

Tickets, camping gear, snacks: who's on it?

How it works: add tasks grouped by phase. Anyone can claim one to own it.

🧭Roles

Driver, navigator, vibe curator. Everyone's got a job.

How it works: one person can hold several roles, and a role can be shared.

🚗Rides

Carpools without the spreadsheet.

How it works: drivers post their car and seat count, crewmates tap to claim a seat. Full cars show Full.

🏕️Stay

Hotel, Airbnb or campsite, sorted.

How it works: post where you're staying and open spots for crewmates to claim.

👗Outfit

Match the theme, or just match the vibe.

How it works: drop photo references so everyone knows the look before the day.

📍Lineup & set meetups

Who's catching which set, and where to find each other.

How it works: the lineup runs in set-time order. Tap 📋 to pick a set, then 📍 to post a meet spot for it.

🗂️Mission Briefing

The whole plan as a swipeable case file: lineup, orders, logistics, meetup, kit, ledger and sign-off. Every page is live, so crewmates' ticks show up as they happen.

Your crew, your plan, one place.

Start it in Secret. Invite them when you're ready.

07 · Lineups

Pick your sets.
Never miss one.

Free interactive lineups, synced with your crew. Vote on must-sees, get reminders, and log who you caught.

🔭Lineup ExplorerFREE

How it works: tap 📋 to pick sets. Log in and your picks sync to your account. Open it →

⭐Crew picks & votes

See where your picks overlap with the crew's.

How it works: tap Start a crew vote. The winners become ⭐ Fam Faves on everyone's schedule.

🔔Set reminders & On deck

A nudge 15 minutes before every set you picked.

How it works: tap 🔔 Remind me once set times drop. On deck shows what's on now and next, and clashes get flagged.

📣Lineup alerts

Know when a fave gets added or set times are posted.

How it works: toggle each alert in Settings. It arrives as a push notification, or by email.

📼Post-fest check-off

"Who'd you catch?" The morning after, log every set.

How it works: tap Saw them or Missed. It all adds to your seen-live count.
