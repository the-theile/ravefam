Your crew · your pace

Start Secret. Go legendary.

Most group tools are useless until everyone joins. RaveFAM flips that — the leader builds the whole thing first, then opens it up when the plan's actually worth sharing. That's the real lifecycle of every crew:

01 · SECRET
Secret

Build the roster and the plan privately. Add your people before anyone signs up — no invites sent, no pressure, no awkward onboarding.

02 · RECRUITING
Recruiting

Flip it live. Share a QR or 6-digit code and members claim their spot in a couple of taps — reviewing what's theirs before anything merges.

03 · LOCKED IN
Locked In

The crew's set. Everyone knows the plan, the meet-up, and the afters — and the whole thing becomes history you keep for next time.

Private by design

Private is a setting you control — not a slogan.

Crew modes decide when you open up. Secret, Recruiting, and Locked In put the visibility switch in your hands.

Private notes are yours alone. Anything you jot on a raver is visible only to you.

No selling, no targeted ads. RaveFAM doesn't sell your personal info or use it to target advertising.

Your number stays off by default. You choose what's shared with the crew — nothing's exposed unless you say so.

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data anytime. Full details live in the Privacy Policy — and since RaveFAM is in beta, we keep the claims honest and specific rather than promising things a product this young can't guarantee.

Questions

Everything else, answered.

What is RaveFAM?

The private home base for your rave crew — build private crews, track festivals and local shows together, and keep the connections and memories that outlast the weekend, all in one place.

Is RaveFAM free to use?

Yes — free to start, no credit card. It's currently in beta, with fast updates as we build alongside our early crews.

How do I join or start a crew?

Scan a QR code or enter a 6-digit claim code to join an existing crew instantly. To start your own, create a crew in under a minute — it begins Secret and private until you're ready to switch it to Recruiting.

Is my crew private?

Every crew starts in Secret mode, visible only to its leader. You control when to open it to Recruiting, and can lock it down once your circle is complete. Private notes are visible only to you.

Do I need to download an app?

No — RaveFAM runs right in your mobile or desktop browser, no app-store download needed. You can add it to your home screen for the full app-like experience.

What about my data and privacy?

RaveFAM doesn't sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Your phone number is off by default, and you can request access, correction, or deletion of your data anytime.
