# DUSK Arizona 2026 × RaveFAM: Instagram campaign

Four carousel posts around DUSK's 10th anniversary (Nov 13–14, Jácome Plaza, Tucson AZ). **Set times weren't out when this was made** (the official site says "about a week prior"), so the campaign leads with the lineup, picks, crew tools and alerts, not set times. Every post sends people to the free DUSK Lineup Explorer (no signup), where saving picks, ♡ and alerts then nudge them to join.

- **Format:** 4:5 feed carousels, 1080×1350 PNG. 11 images: 3 a post for posts 1–3, 2 for post 4.
- **Suggested time:** 6–7 PM MST (Arizona) on the post date. Post 3 goes up Friday morning, before doors.
- **Names:** real festival and artist names as they appear on the Lineup Explorer, as text only. There are no artist photos or logos. Crewmates (Sam, Kai, Jess, Theo), the "Desert Rats" crew and the want-to-see counts are demo data.
- **Screenshots:** real explorer and app screens captured with demo data (`_build/`).
- **Link in bio:** `https://myravefam.com/lineup-explorer/dusk-arizona-2026`

## Notes

- **Feature accuracy (v2.34.0):** every claim is a live feature. Set-times alerts fire from the `afa_lineup_alerts_set_times` trigger once DUSK times land in the database, for users Going or Interested; they need a login and notification permission (otherwise email). Offline: the explorer saves a logged-in member's picks and favorites per festival (`rf_lp_offline`) and the service worker keeps the page. No On deck, set reminders or clash slides: those need set times.
- **Lineup:** 42 artists as of Nov 10 (Soaplab dropped off the official lineup). Captions name only headliners.
- **Before posting:** check the handle, the link in bio, and that horsegiirL's day hasn't moved (official: Saturday).
- **Regenerating:** captures come from `_build/capture.spec.js`; copy it into `tests/` and run it with Playwright, chromium only (in the cloud sandbox: `PW_CHROME=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`). Slides come from `node marketing/dusk-arizona-2026/_build/slides.mjs` (same `PW_CHROME`). It expects the Orbitron, Space Grotesk and Urbanist woff2 files plus a `local.css` with their `@font-face` rules in `_build/fonts/` (Google Fonts, OFL, not committed).
