# Hulaween 2026 × RaveFAM: 5-day Instagram campaign

Five carousel posts for the five days before Hulaween (Oct 22–25, Live Oak, FL). Each post shows off one part of the new Lineup Explorer ↔ RaveFAM features. Every post sends people to the free Lineup Explorer (no signup), where saving picks, ♡ and reminders then nudge them to join.

- **Format:** 4:5 feed carousels, 1080×1350 PNG. 14 images: 3 a day, 2 on day 5.
- **Suggested time:** 6–7 PM ET each day, when festival-goers are planning.
- **Names:** real festival and artist names as they appear on the Lineup Explorer, as text only. There are no artist photos or logos. Crewmates (Sam, Kai, Jess, Theo) are demo data.
- **Screenshots:** real explorer and app screens captured with demo data (`_build/`). Days 3 and 5 include app screens.
- **Link in bio:** `https://myravefam.com/lineup-explorer/hulaween-2026`

## Notes

- **Feature accuracy:** every claim above is a live feature as of v1.77.0. On deck and reminders need set times, which Hulaween has. Reminders and alerts need a login and notification permission.
- **Before posting:** check the handle and the link-in-bio. If you'd rather not name Pretty Lights and Green Velvet in the Day 3 caption, swap in "two headliners".
- **Regenerating:** captures come from `_build/capture.spec.js`; copy it into `tests/` and run it with Playwright, chromium only. Slides come from `node marketing/hulaween-2026/_build/slides.mjs`. It expects the Orbitron, Space Grotesk and Urbanist woff2 files, plus a `local.css` with their `@font-face` rules, in `_build/fonts/`. These are Google Fonts (OFL) and aren't committed.
