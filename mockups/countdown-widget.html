Home Screen Countdown

A tiny public page per crew, myravefam.com/countdown/<token>, that shows days until the crew's next RSVP'd festival. It works as a Safari home-screen icon, inside a Scriptable widget, or as a lock-screen glance. Three looks, the edge-case states, the in-app entry point and the setup guide are below.

Each look is shown at iOS small (170²) and medium (364×170). Pick one and it becomes the route's only style. Crew colour drives the accent.

Open questions

Which look?A big number, B laminate, C ticket stub, or a mix (e.g. A for widgets, B for the full page).
Per-crew links, all-crews link, or both?Per-crew is simpler and easier to share. All-crews is a per-raver token.
"Next festival" = ?The crew's soonest rave where ≥1 member RSVP'd? Only raves the viewer RSVP'd to? Or one the crew has pinned?
Who can make or revoke a link?Any member, or crew admins only?
Show who's going?Initials or a count on the public page, or keep it anonymous?
Real widget pathScriptable for now (zero native work). A native WidgetKit widget would be a batched iOS release later.
