Crew page — five ways in

Today the crew page is an Overview / Roster switch, a Share button, and a 4×2 grid of equal-weight tiles (Huddle, Pins, Dream Board, Poll, Jams, Archive, History, Rave Plan) that open accordion panels. Each direction below gives Huddle and Rave Plan the most weight and reorganizes the other six. Each phone is lightly clickable.

All five use the same groupings: Polls + Jams + Dream Board become one Vibes / "what we're hyping" group, and Pins + Archive + History become one Memories group. Roster moves out of a tab and into the avatar stack in the header (tap → member sheet with Invite, Status, Edit/Leave). Rave Plan is still conditional: when there's no shared upcoming rave, its slot becomes an "Add your next rave" prompt and never leaves an empty space.
A · Huddle Stage

The chat is the crew page

The top of the page is a live Huddle with the last few messages, room pills and a composer you can type into. Below it sits the Rave Plan ticket. The other six areas become a horizontal "stash" rail that opens one panel at a time.

B · Crew Dock

A crew gets its own tab bar

Opening a crew swaps the app's bottom nav for a crew dock: Huddle · Plan · Vibes · Memories · Fam. Five destinations instead of eight tiles, each a full screen rather than an accordion. Tap the dock to switch.
