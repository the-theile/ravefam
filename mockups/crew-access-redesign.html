Crew page — five ways in (v2, real screen)

Revised against the live screen: every phone now keeps the RaveFAM header and the Crews / Raves / Ravers / Stats / Village nav, uses an 8-person crew, and starts from what's actually on the page today. E · Crew Feed is the leading direction and comes first. Each phone is lightly clickable.

Shared changes across all five
  • Who's Going + Rave Plan become one ticket: the paper card keeps the avatars, adds the countdown, and its perforated stub shows checklist progress and opens the plan.
  • Duplicates removed: "Share Crew Card" becomes an icon in the crew header; "Back to My Crews" becomes the ‹ beside the totem.
  • Roster moves behind the avatar stack (tap → members, Invite, Status, Edit/Leave). Overview/Roster tabs go away.
  • Groups: Polls + Jams + Dream Board = Vibes; Pins + Archive + History = Memories.
  • With no shared upcoming rave, the ticket becomes "Pick your next rave together", never an empty slot.
