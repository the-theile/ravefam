Crew Feed — Aurora Glass + ticket stub

Variation 2's frosted-glass feed with the Backstage Pass paper ticket for the countdown. The same design in three states. Filter chips and the + button work in every phone.

  • The ticket stays paper on the glass page. Its side notches are cut out so the glow shows through, and it casts a soft pink glow so it sits in the glass world without looking pasted on.
  • Plan check-offs and badges use small paper cards in the feed, so crew milestones read like the ticket. Everything else (polls, jams, dreams, archive) stays glass.
  • Huddle is the glass pill with a typing indicator and unread count. The + opens a glass menu to start a poll, jam, dream or archive link.
