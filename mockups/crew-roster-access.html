Where does Roster go?

Today a toggle switches between Overview and Roster. The Roster side holds: Share Crew Card + Invite Link, add a member, the Crew Manifest (members), crew Status (Recruiting / Locked In), and Edit / Delete (lead) or Leave / Report (members). The new feed design replaces Overview, so Roster needs a home. Three options, all in the Aurora Glass + ticket style, shown from the lead's view.

Same content in all three, reordered by how often it's used: Invite and Share first, then the member list (here-now dots, LEAD tag, "raves together" count, a ⋯ menu per member for the lead), then Status with its meaning, then Edit / Delete last. Members who aren't the lead see Leave / Report there instead.
A · Manifest sheet

Tap the faces, the crew slides up

No toggle at all. The avatar stack in the header gets a "8 ›" label so it reads as a button, and opens a tall glass sheet over the feed. Swipe down or tap outside to close. The feed underneath never moves.

Try: tap the avatars or ✕
Why: roster is a "sometimes" place; a sheet keeps it one tap away without spending permanent space on a toggle. Watch: it's only as discoverable as the avatar button, so the "8 ›" label matters.

C · Flip the pass

Turn the crew pass over

The crew header becomes a glass pass card with "Flip for crew ↻". Tapping it flips the card over to its paper back: the Crew Manifest with barcode, like today's manifest. Status, invites and settings sit below it.

Try: tap the card to flip
Why: keeps the Backstage Pass / laminate identity and today's manifest + barcode, and it's memorable. Watch: a flip is a novel gesture; the "Flip for crew" label has to carry it.
