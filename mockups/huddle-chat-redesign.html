Huddle Chat — three visual directions

Same structure in all three: a full-screen chat surface replacing the accordion panel, with the conversation owning the viewport. Only the visual treatment differs. Pick one (or mix — e.g. "B's bubbles, A's composer") and I'll build it into app.html.

Shared across all three: sticky header with room switcher + presence · pinned strip · “Load earlier” pagination · day separators · sender grouping (one avatar per run) · per-message timestamps · delivery ticks · “New messages” unread divider · reply-with-quote · @mentions · typing indicator · one-line composer [+] [input] [➤] · reactions tucked under the bubble · per-person sender colours · fixed voice-note duration (1:24, not 0:84).
