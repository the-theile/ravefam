RaveFAM is a private planner for rave crews: friends form a crew, track which festivals and
shows each person is going to, plan the weekend together (Game Plan, polls, Dream Board) and
chat in a crew group chat (Huddle). It's for adults (18+) and free, with no purchases.

SIGNING IN
Sign-in is by email code (no password). Use the demo account in the Sign-in fields; it is
already in a crew with upcoming raves, so every tab has content. Tap "Log in", enter the
email, and enter the code given in the Sign-in fields.

NATIVE FEATURES (beyond the website)
- Push notifications (APNs) for crew Beacons, @mentions, set-time reminders and lineup alerts,
  plus the app icon badge for unread Huddle messages
- Haptic feedback on RSVPs, votes, reactions, sends and the Radar Map (Settings → Haptics)
- Native share sheet for crew invites and rave cards; long-press a card image to save it to Photos
- QR scanner (camera) to join a crew from a friend's code
- Voice notes in Huddle (microphone)
- Nearby raves using device location (stays on the device; never uploaded)
- Universal Links: myravefam.com/rave/... and invite links open in the app
- Offline screen when there's no connection

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
- Terms (Settings → Terms of Service) forbid objectionable content and abusive users;
  users agree to them at sign-up.
- Report: content and profiles have a Report option in their ⋯ menu (for a Huddle message,
  long-press it, then ⋯ → Report), with categories such as harassment, inappropriate content
  and safety.
- Block: open a raver's profile → ⋯ → Block. Their messages and notifications disappear for
  the blocker immediately.
- Filter: posts, profile text, crew names and reviews containing blocked terms are rejected
  by the server before they're saved.
- Moderation: reports go to a moderator dashboard. We act on reports within
  [24 hours] by removing content and restricting or removing the user.
- Contact: bump@myravefam.com

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Settings (gear icon) → Delete my account. It permanently erases the profile, photos and
account in the app; no email or website step is needed.

PRIVACY
Settings → Privacy Policy. Location is used only to sort nearby raves and stays on the device.
No tracking and no third-party ad or analytics SDKs.

PAYMENTS
None. The app is free and has no purchases, tips or external payment links.
